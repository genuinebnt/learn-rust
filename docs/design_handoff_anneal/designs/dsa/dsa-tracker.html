THE RAIL SWAPS

Filters (or press f) replaces the Activity panels in the right rail with one long card per filter; Activity brings them back. Every count already accounts for the other filters. Applying a filter doesn't hide the rail's own state: the tab shows how many are active.

PATTERNS ↔ PROBLEMS

The grid is the catalog you know, one card per NeetCode pattern, with its lesson one click away. Switch to problems for long cards: each must-learn problem carries the practice problems that reuse its idea underneath it.

GOALS ARE SHORTCUTS

The three goal cards double as list switches: NeetCode 150, the new ideas in the 250 (must-learn problems the 150 doesn't cover), and All. Blind 75 and the full 250 are in the list chips.
